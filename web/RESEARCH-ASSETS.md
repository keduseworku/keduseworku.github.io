# Original assets in this portfolio

All scientific assets are displayed intact, with original labels, axes, legends, and units. These are figures from papers coauthored by Keduse Worku, included at the user's request. They are distinct from the illustrative WebGL scenes.

- `public/media/keduse-worku.jpg`: portrait from https://keduseworku.github.io/assets/img/prof_pic.jpg. Original dimensions 1144 × 1092. No photographer credit was listed on the source page.
- `public/media/macs0647-field.jpg`: Worku et al. (2025), Figure 1, left panel. Original asset: https://arxiv.org/html/2512.11985v1/macs0647_labeled_high-z.jpg. Caption: https://arxiv.org/html/2512.11985v1#S1.F1. 1206 × 1991.
- `public/media/cosmic-dawn-21cm.png`: Worku, Cruz & Kamionkowski (2026), Figure 2. Original asset: https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05323v1/figs/T21_delta21_p2.png. Caption: https://arxiv.org/html/2605.05323v1#S2.F2. 1900 × 620.

The source papers list the arXiv perpetual non-exclusive distribution license, not a Creative Commons license. No scientific data or imagery has been regenerated or recolored.
