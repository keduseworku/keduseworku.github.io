02 / MACS0647 · GRAVITATIONAL LENSING

A closer look atdistant galaxies.

I’ve also worked with JWST observations. My collaborators and I studied distant galaxies behind a cluster called MACS0647, which magnifies their light.

The cluster’s gravity bends the light on its way to us. That helps us see faint galaxies, though it also stretches and distorts what we’re looking at.

We used images to find galaxy candidates, then spectroscopy to confirm redshifts. The distinction matters: a promising candidate still needs to be checked.
